# Stakeholder & Business Requirements

## Stakeholder Map

### Credit/Risk
**Need:** Understand portfolio risk and identify potentially higher-risk segments.

**Questions:**
- Which characteristics are associated with default?
- How stable are these patterns?
- How should risk be monitored?

### Lending
**Need:** Support consistent assessment of loan applications.

**Questions:**
- Can analytics help prioritise applications for additional review?
- What is the impact of false positives and false negatives?

### Management
**Need:** Understand business performance and risk at a high level.

**Questions:**
- What are the main risk indicators?
- What KPIs should be monitored?
- How might changes in risk affect the lending portfolio?

### Data/Analytics
**Need:** Build, test and monitor reliable analytical solutions.

**Questions:**
- Is the data suitable?
- Which model performs best for the stated objective?
- How should performance be monitored?

## Business Requirements

1. Produce clear risk-related KPIs.
2. Identify patterns associated with loan default.
3. Build a predictive model for risk classification.
4. Evaluate the model using more than accuracy alone.
5. Translate technical findings into business language.
6. Present findings in a form suitable for management reporting.
7. Clearly document limitations and responsible-use considerations.
